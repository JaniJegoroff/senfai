Privacy policy

SenFai works offline. It has no account, advertising SDK, custom analytics or tracking.

Effective 18 October 2026 · Covers the free 44-puzzle iPhone release.

Who is responsible?

SenFai: Line Puzzles is provided by Jani Jegoroff. For privacy questions or a request about information you have sent to support, email jani.jegoroff@gmail.com.

Information in the game

The game does not send your puzzle progress or settings to the developer. It does not ask you to create an account or provide a name, email address, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier.

Completed puzzle IDs, your selected level, tutorial status and preferences are stored locally on your device so the game can remember them. An unfinished drawing is held in memory while the app remains open. SenFai does not run a server for game saves and does not sync progress between devices.

Storage and deletion

Local saved progress and settings remain until the app’s data is removed. Deleting SenFai removes its local data; this also removes your completed-puzzle progress. Offloading an app is different from deleting it and may preserve its data. Any device backup copies and restoration are managed by Apple under your device and backup settings. The developer cannot retrieve or delete those copies remotely.

If you contact support

Contacting support is optional and separate from playing. If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and whatever message, device details or attachments you choose to include. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the problem, not for advertising or mailing lists.

Support correspondence is processed by the email services used to send and receive it. It is kept only for as long as needed to handle the request and necessary follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask for access, correction or deletion by emailing the address above. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Apple services and TestFlight

Apple handles App Store downloads, device backups and optional system diagnostics under its own policies and your settings. Apple may provide the developer with store statistics and diagnostic reports. If you use TestFlight, Apple also provides beta-testing information and feedback according to the TestFlight terms and your choices. SenFai does not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

See Apple’s privacy information.

This support website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The site itself uses no advertising, analytics scripts, cookies, contact forms or third-party fonts. When you visit a page, GitHub processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the website under its own policies. This website traffic is separate from the offline game.

See GitHub’s privacy statement. Choosing an email link opens your email application; no message is sent unless you send it.

Sharing and changes

The developer does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Information voluntarily sent to support is not shared for marketing. It may be processed by the service providers described above or disclosed where legally required.

If the game’s features or privacy practices change, this policy and the relevant App Store disclosures will be reviewed and updated. The current release has no paid packs or in-app purchases.
