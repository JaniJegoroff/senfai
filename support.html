How do I complete a puzzle?

Connect each pair of matching dots and fill every cell. Move up, down, left or right; paths cannot cross. Both the connected-pair count and the filled percentage above the board must be complete. If every pair is connected but cells are empty, redraw a path through those cells.

Can I undo or draw from both ends?

Trace backward to erase part of your line. You can lift your finger, draw from the other matching dot and join the two loose tips. Touching an endpoint of a completed pair restarts that pair. Restart clears the current drawing so you can try again.

Are all the puzzles free?

Yes. All 44 original puzzles are free, including 32 Main puzzles and 12 Challenge puzzles. Every puzzle is selectable and replay is unlimited. There are no ads, subscriptions or purchases in this release.

What progress is saved?

Completed puzzles, the selected level, tutorial status and settings are saved on your device. An unfinished drawing stays while the app remains open; if iOS ends the app or you close it from the app switcher, that board starts fresh. Completed puzzles remain marked.

SenFai does not have an account or cloud-sync service. Progress does not sync between devices. Deleting the app removes its local save; do not delete it as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep your progress. Device backups and restoration are controlled by Apple and are not a SenFai recovery service.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The puzzles, artwork and sounds are included in the app. No internet connection is needed to play. Viewing this website, contacting support and downloading app updates use your internet connection.

How do I change sounds or make pairs easier to identify?

Open Settings to turn on Sound, Completion haptics or Pair symbols. Symbols add matching shapes to dots and unfinished line tips. SenFai follows your iPhone’s text-size and Reduce Motion settings. Gameplay requires drawing on the visual board; VoiceOver gameplay is not currently supported.

Why is there a save warning?

If the latest progress could not be saved, use Retry save when shown. If a saved record could not be opened, SenFai avoids overwriting it; close and reopen the app once, then contact support if the warning remains. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot a save warning.

Which devices and languages are supported?

SenFai is made for iPhone running iOS 16 or later. The interface and instructions are in English. The current release has no Game Center, multiplayer or cross-device progress.
